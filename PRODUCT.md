# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Visitors evaluating adore_blvnk’s identity and work: collaborators, technical peers, prospective clients, and recruiters. They should understand the persona quickly and reach verified contact channels without navigating a conventional portfolio grid.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio and identity surface that presents adore_blvnk as a CLI-focused software architect through one memorable interactive specimen. Success means the character, phrase, and contact routes remain legible while the site feels authored rather than templated.

## Brand Personality

Severe, technical, and subcultural. The emotional target is controlled intensity: monochrome Swiss/HUD precision shaped by UK drill imagery, without becoming a generic terminal theme.

## Anti-references

Generic SaaS landing pages, cute or mascot-like 3D characters, glossy cyber-armour, decorative point clouds, soft glass cards, warm editorial templates, and interaction that sacrifices the character’s pose or hand sign.

## Design Principles

- Make one identity-bearing specimen the visual anchor.
- Preserve meaning through every treatment; dithering must not erase anatomy or gesture.
- Use technical overlays as spatial evidence, not decoration.
- Keep interactions decisive and sparse; scroll velocity may modulate entropy without controlling layout.
- Keep content and contact routes usable through WebGL context recovery.
