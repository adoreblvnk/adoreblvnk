# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Recruiters, potential collaborators, technical peers, and readers evaluating Joseph's engineering judgment, technical identity, and body of work.

## Product Purpose

Present adore_blvnk as a distinctive software architect and CLI-focused engineer. The portfolio should establish technical credibility quickly, make writings and contact routes easy to reach, and leave a memorable identity without behaving like a generic résumé template.

## Brand Personality

Controlled, mechanical, and severe. The experience should feel authored, technically capable, and deliberate rather than decorative or trend-led.

## Anti-references

Do not copy Sananes' or AstroDither's surface aesthetics. Avoid generic RGB aberration, persistent CRT overlays, dashboard-like metadata, fluid-demo spectacle, bloom-heavy graphics, continuous soundtracks, and motion added independently to every element. Avoid conventional portfolio card grids and generic developer-terminal styling.

## Design Principles

- Make the portrait and identity the dominant signal.
- Coordinate motion as one system rather than a collection of effects.
- Use restraint and quiet intervals so moments of intensity remain meaningful.
- Let technical behavior demonstrate capability instead of explaining it with excess interface chrome.
- Preserve source identity and readability before introducing variation or distortion.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use full motion by default as an intentional part of the identity. Keep sound opt-in and activate audio only after explicit user interaction. Maintain keyboard access, visible focus states, semantic content structure, and strong achromatic contrast. Never make sound necessary to understand or operate the site.
